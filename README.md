# despegate

> *Despégate* (Spanish): "unglue yourself".

Intrusive alerts that force you to stop what you are doing when it is time to leave.

## Why

Normal reminders are easy to dismiss when you are deep into a game or any other absorbing task: you click the notification away, keep going, and end up late. despegate is for people who need something that actually interrupts them — alerts that cannot be ignored or silently dismissed.

## Status

Early planning. Nothing is usable yet.

## Planned

- **Desktop (PC)** — alerts that take over the screen, including on top of fullscreen games, and escalate until you really stop.
- **Mobile** — a companion version with the same idea.
